🧑‍💻 About USB CoverFix Deluxe

Hi, I'm Siegfried 👋 – the indie dev behind this little tool.

It all started with a simple annoyance: I wanted nice album covers for my car stereo, but editing hundreds of MP3s by hand was a nightmare. So I built USB CoverFix Deluxe – first for myself, then for friends, and now for you.

What makes it special? Besides batch processing and a built-in player, there's a tiny AI fairy 🧚 who helps you out when you're stuck. She's a bit cheeky, but always useful.

The tool is intentionally simple: no bloat, no hidden fees. The trial lets you edit 20 MP3s per day for free – plenty of time to see if it fits your workflow. If it saves you time and you'd like to support me, I'd be thrilled if you buy me a virtual coffee ☕ or grab the full version.

Thanks for stopping by – and enjoy USB CoverFix Deluxe!
